# Lian Character Card — Project Map

This folder contains the current Janitor AI character-card work. Keep one canonical card and avoid maintaining parallel full-card drafts.

## Active files

- **`aunt-character-card-final.md`** — the single active card draft. This is the source to edit when changing the card.
- **`lian-scenario.md`** — detailed backstory, chronology, and starting situation.
- **`aunt-character-notes.md`** — current design decisions, character priorities, and deferred work.

## Current direction

Lian is an adult, mature, naturally feminine, emotionally expressive woman. Her femininity should be felt as part of her physical presence and everyday behavior, not merely listed as a personality trait. She should remain lively and individual without constantly performing sweetness, flirtation, or beauty.

The exact portrayal of this presence is still being developed. Do not treat experimental scene examples or old dialogue sets as approved references.

## Deliberately omitted for now

- A separate **Speech Style** section.
- **Example Dialogues**. New examples will be developed later after the desired voice and presence are clearer.
- Alternate opening-message variants. The main card retains one working First Message.

## Maintenance rules

1. Edit the canonical card rather than creating another numbered full-card draft.
2. Keep scenario chronology in `lian-scenario.md` and avoid duplicating it in design notes.
3. Update the design notes when priorities change.
4. Only add dialogue examples after they have been reviewed and accepted; do not preserve rejected examples as if they were canonical.
